# Notification Thresholds

## Immediate notification

- Official OpenAI mechanism changes
- Official incident acknowledgement
- Official rollback or restore
- Stable/service shipped releases
- Model availability changes
- Plan or credit eligibility changes

## Community evidence

B-level events require independent evidence. Same reporter or same mechanism follow-ups do not automatically count as independent.

## Boundaries

- CLI stable does not imply Desktop or Work.
- Third-party trackers cannot confirm official completion.
- A maintainer requesting logs does not equal acknowledgement.
- User speculation is not direct routing evidence.

## Lifecycle

Track:

teaser → announcement → rollout-start → completion

Do not skip stages without evidence.
